about

projector. was formed in the northern suburbs of Adelaide in April 2026 by four mates with one thing in common: a love of rock music. The band's sound is versatile: an eclectic blend of indie rock, classic rock, alternative, and synth pop.

With an audience and fan-focused philosophy, they endeavour to write crowd-pleasers that involve the audience just as much as themselves as artists.
